KuTasks Privacy Policy

Application: KuTasks (com.kutasks.app) • Platforms: Android 13+ & Windows 10+ Desktop • Effective Date: October 7, 2026 • Version: 1.0.0

🛡️ Plain-English Summary (Data Sovereignty First)

KuTasks is designed from the ground up as an offline-first, local sovereignty productivity application.

  • We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit your personal data or task contents to any remote cloud servers.
  • Zero Accounts: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide an email address to use KuTasks.
  • Zero Trackers: There are no advertising SDKs, no behavioral analytics (no Google Firebase, no Crashlytics, no telemetry).
  • Local Wi-Fi Only Sync: Peer-to-peer device sync operates strictly over your private local Wi-Fi network without external cloud relays.
  • Full Data Ownership: All tasks, notes, habits, and preferences reside solely on your local device storage in a local SQLite database.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because KuTasks operates on a strict local-first model, we do not collect or process any of the following categories of information:

  • Personal Identifiers: Names, email addresses, phone numbers, postal addresses, or account usernames.
  • Device Identifiers & Advertising IDs: We do not collect Google Advertising IDs (GAID), Android IDs, MAC addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • Telemetry & Usage Analytics: We do not track screen views, button clicks, usage times, or behavioral telemetry.
  • Crash Logs: Crash reports and system logs remain on your device and are never automatically transmitted over the network.
  • Location Data: KuTasks never requests or accesses GPS or precise device geolocation.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data created inside KuTasks is stored locally on your device within the application's sandboxed storage directory in an offline SQLite database (on-device storage):

  • Tasks & Subtasks: Task titles, markdown notes, due dates/times, completion statuses, priority levels, and subtask checklists.
  • Organization: Projects, tags, and MoSCoW agile classification tiers (Must, Should, Could, Won't).
  • Habits & Routines: Daily checklist items, routine completion histories, and daily streak counts.
  • Focus Logs: Pomodoro session timestamps and focus duration totals.
  • Preferences: Theme selections (Espresso, Porcelain, AMOLED, Accent Palettes), sound settings, and notification configurations stored in local key-value preferences.

This data is strictly private and never leaves your device unless you initiate a local Wi-Fi sync transfer to another device you own or perform a manual file export.

3. Peer-to-Peer (P2P) Local Wi-Fi Synchronization

KuTasks includes an optional peer-to-peer sync feature that enables seamless synchronization between your Android smartphone and Windows Desktop PC. Here is exactly how this operates:

  • Local Subnet Only: Devices discover each other using local Multicast DNS (mDNS) with the service type _kutasks._tcp. This discovery occurs strictly within your private local Wi-Fi router / Local Area Network (LAN).
  • Direct WebSockets (Port 9847): Once discovered, devices establish a direct peer-to-peer WebSocket connection on port 9847.
  • Zero Cloud Intermediaries: Your data travels directly from Device A to Device B over your local home or office router. No cloud servers, external brokers, or relay nodes are ever contacted. If your Wi-Fi router has no internet connection, local sync continues to function completely without interruption.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

KuTasks contains zero third-party tracking, advertising, or analytics SDKs. Specifically:

  • No Google Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Cloud Messaging.
  • No Facebook / Meta SDK or ad libraries.
  • No third-party ad networks (e.g., Google AdMob, Unity Ads).
  • All application components and storage engines operate purely on-device without remote telemetric reporting or cloud tracking.

6. Data Retention, Portability, and Deletion

You have complete control and total ownership of your data at all times:

  • Data Export: You can export your tasks and projects at any time using the built-in Data Portability tools in Markdown (with Obsidian frontmatter), RFC 4180 CSV, JSON snapshot, or atomic SQLite database snapshot.
  • Data Deletion: You can permanently delete all data at any moment by navigating to your Android device settings → Apps → KuTasks → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application. Because no data is stored on remote servers, deleting local app data erases all data irreversibly and immediately.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

KuTasks does not knowingly collect, request, or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). Because the application collects zero personal information from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.
